# Sentinel avatar prompt

Create a finished, exceptionally crafted square brand avatar, 1024 × 1024, for a contemporary digital observation tool. The entire image is visual: absolutely no letters, words, numbers, pseudo-typography, tiny labels, watermarks or signatures.

Art direction: premium experimental early-2000s digital graphic design, Swiss editorial precision and Y2K chrome object photography. A clean warm-white paper-like field, one bold flat ultramarine/cobalt-blue rectangular plane behind the object, sparse black hairline fragments and deliberate asymmetry at the edges. Strict palette: white, mirror silver, black reflections and deep cobalt blue only. Reference the visual language of polished chrome sculptures floating above sharply cropped cobalt graphic blocks; do not include chess pieces, hands or copied logos. Sophisticated, confident, quiet, minimal. No cyberpunk neon, rainbow gradients, glow haze, gaming badge, futuristic HUD, interface cards, stars or ornamental clutter.

Central object: one original sculptural MIRROR-CHROME SHIELD, shown nearly straight-on with only a slight three-dimensional turn. A broad upper edge, gently rounded shoulders, smooth tapering sides, and a single clean lower point. The exterior silhouette should be simple and robust, immediately recognizable at 48 pixels. The upper third of the shield contains exactly ONE narrow, straight, horizontal dark-cobalt recessed slit, with a crisp machined edge. It is an abstract observation aperture, NOT a face, NOT a literal human eye, NOT a helmet: no pupils, eyebrows, nose, mouth, eye symbols or anthropomorphic features. No keyhole, padlock, checkmark, military emblem, police badge, crest, heraldry, crown, laurel, weapons or text. The shield is a single continuous solid sculptural object with a lightly convex polished face, substantial but refined beveled perimeter, flawless machining and elegant proportions.

Material and lighting: physically convincing highly polished silver chrome, precise white specular strips, deep black reflected areas and restrained cobalt reflection from the backing plane. Shape and volume come from meticulously controlled studio reflections, not glowing outlines. Avoid excessive small facets or reflections that make the silhouette noisy. A restrained soft cast shadow separates the object from the flat background.

Composition and cropping: center the main shield horizontally and vertically. Keep the ENTIRE shield, including every shoulder, bevel and lower point, within the central 65% of the canvas in both axes, with generous uncluttered white breathing room. It must survive Telegram's circular crop without clipping any part of the shield. The cobalt rectangle is slightly offset behind the shield, wide enough to provide contrast, but it never cuts across or covers the shield's foreground silhouette. Only two or three very fine black geometric line fragments may sit at the outer margins, secondary to the single central icon. The composition must stay clear and calm when reduced to a small round avatar. Museum-quality product rendering joined to expertly balanced print-style collage. Deliver the finished square image only, fully opaque background.
