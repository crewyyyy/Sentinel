# Sentinel banner prompt

Create a meticulously art-directed original brand banner for a Telegram bot named SENTINEL. Landscape canvas, requested 1536 × 1024. Visual language: early-2000s experimental digital collage combined with rigorous Swiss editorial typography and photorealistic mirror-chrome product sculpture. White/off-white ground; deep saturated cobalt blue geometric rectangular planes; restrained black hairline geometry. Think chrome object on a precisely cut graphic collage, not a generic cybersecurity advertisement.

Main object on the RIGHT: one sculptural mirror-chrome shield, broad curved upper shoulders, gently convex solid surface, tapering cleanly to one lower point. Its upper third has exactly ONE thin straight horizontal dark-cobalt slit, recessed into the metal, a minimal abstract signal of observation. This is a neutral geometric brand emblem, NOT a helmet, face, eye, mask, padlock, police badge, military insignia or weapon. Keep the shield entirely visible. Beautiful controlled black reflections, bright white specular edge highlights, believable polished metal, exceptionally clean modeled edges, slight three-quarter depth. No colored neon outline and no glowing sci-fi UI.

On the LEFT, the only text anywhere in the entire image: SENTINEL, spelled exactly in uppercase, bold black modern grotesk, one single line, elegantly tracked and fully readable at phone scale. Make it a substantial editorial wordmark balanced against the chrome emblem, with ample white space and NO overlapping graphics on its letters. No subtitle, no slogan, no microtext, no technical labels, no numbers, no decorative pseudo-writing, no watermark.

Behind the shield, a compact asymmetrical collage of two or three cobalt-blue block shapes, one white cutout plane and perhaps one black rectangular fragment. A few razor-thin black horizontal/diagonal rules and a very restrained cropped circular curve may provide direction and tension, not clutter. Use solid planes and carefully separated layers rather than gradients. Reference character: chrome chess-sculpture poster meets blue-and-white experimental Y2K collage, but create a wholly original shield identity without borrowing any reference text or symbols. Do not include a chess piece, people or hands.

Composition must function as a wide Telegram menu banner. Keep ALL essential content (the full wordmark and the full shield) in the central vertical band between 16% and 84% of canvas height so a centered crop to 16:9 remains usable; top and bottom should be quiet spacious white breathing room with only nonessential geometric accents. Maintain comfortable outer margins. Shield around the right 62–85% of width, wordmark around left 9–54%, with clean separation. Large clear shapes, minimal details, precisely balanced visual weight. Finish like an expertly crafted contemporary graphic design print: crisp typography, crisp cut edges, fine chrome reflections, no excessive decoration, no blur, no grunge filter.
